## Q1 — Verbal Analogy (Verbal battery)
**Prompt:** PEN is to WRITE as BROOM is to ___?
- A) Sweep  ✅
- B) Bristles
- C) Floor
- D) Dust

**Answer:** A) Sweep
**Explanation:** A pen's job is to write; a broom's job is to sweep — tool to the action it performs.
**Tier:** Easy · **Countdown:** 5s

## Q2 — Sentence Completion (Verbal battery)
**Prompt:** The bridge was too weak to hold the heavy truck, so workers had to ___ it before opening the road.
- A) decorate
- B) reinforce  ✅
- C) rename
- D) measure

**Answer:** B) reinforce
**Explanation:** "Too weak" needs strengthening — only *reinforce* fixes the problem the sentence describes.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 6s

## Q3 — Number Series (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** What comes next? 3, 6, 12, 24, ___
- A) 36
- B) 48  ✅
- C) 30
- D) 42

**Answer:** B) 48
**Explanation:** Each number doubles (×2): 3→6→12→24, so the next is 24 × 2 = 48.
**Tier:** Easy · **Countdown:** 5s

## Q4 — Number Analogy (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** 12 → 6, 20 → 10, 8 → 4, so 30 → ___?
- A) 12
- B) 15  ✅
- C) 20
- D) 25

**Answer:** B) 15
**Explanation:** Each number is cut in half (÷2): 12→6, 20→10, 8→4, so 30 ÷ 2 = 15.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 6s

## Q5 — Figure Series (Nonverbal battery — render as shape icons)
**Prompt:** The shapes gain one side each step: triangle, square, pentagon, ___. Which comes next?
- A) Circle
- B) Hexagon  ✅
- C) Square
- D) Octagon

**Answer:** B) Hexagon
**Explanation:** Sides count up by one: triangle 3 → square 4 → pentagon 5 → hexagon 6.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 6s
