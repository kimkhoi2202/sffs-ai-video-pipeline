## Q1 — Verbal Analogy (Verbal battery)
**Prompt:** OCEAN is to PUDDLE as MOUNTAIN is to ___?
- A) Valley
- B) Hill  ✅
- C) River
- D) Rock

**Answer:** B) Hill
**Explanation (1-line):** Ocean → puddle is huge → tiny of the same kind; a mountain's tiny version is a hill.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 6s

## Q2 — Verbal Classification (Verbal battery)
**Prompt:** Which one does NOT belong? Robin · Sparrow · Salmon · Eagle
- A) Robin
- B) Sparrow
- C) Salmon  ✅
- D) Eagle

**Answer:** C) Salmon
**Explanation:** Robin, sparrow, and eagle are birds; a salmon is a fish.
**Tier:** Easy · **Countdown:** 5s

## Q3 — Number Series (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** What comes next? 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ___
- A) 36
- B) 40
- C) 42  ✅
- D) 44

**Answer:** C) 42
**Explanation:** The gaps grow +4, +6, +8, +10 … so the next gap is +12 → 30 + 12 = 42.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 7s

## Q4 — Number Analogy (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** 2 → 4, 3 → 9, 4 → 16, so 5 → ___?
- A) 10
- B) 20
- C) 25  ✅
- D) 32

**Answer:** C) 25
**Explanation:** Each number is squared (2²=4, 3²=9, 4²=16), so 5² = 25.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 6s

## Q5 — Figure Series / Rotation (Nonverbal battery — render as arrow icons)
**Prompt:** An arrow points UP, then RIGHT, then DOWN. Which way does it point next?
- A) Left  ✅
- B) Up
- C) Right
- D) Down

**Answer:** A) Left
**Explanation:** The arrow makes a quarter-turn clockwise each step: up → right → down → left.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 6s
**Render note:** Show as rotating arrow icons on screen (a nonverbal/spatial item), not as text options.
