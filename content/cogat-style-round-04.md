## Q1 — Verbal Classification (Verbal battery)
**Prompt:** Which one does NOT belong? Oak · Pine · Maple · Rose
- A) Oak
- B) Pine
- C) Maple
- D) Rose  ✅

**Answer:** D) Rose
**Explanation:** Oak, pine, and maple are trees; a rose is a flower.
**Tier:** Easy · **Countdown:** 5s

## Q2 — Verbal Analogy (Verbal battery)
**Prompt:** PAINTER is to BRUSH as CARPENTER is to ___?
- A) Hammer  ✅
- B) Wood
- C) House
- D) Nail

**Answer:** A) Hammer
**Explanation:** A painter's tool is a brush; a carpenter's tool is a hammer (wood is material, a house is the product).
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 6s

## Q3 — Number Series (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** What comes next? 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ___
- A) 11
- B) 13  ✅
- C) 12
- D) 16

**Answer:** B) 13
**Explanation:** Each number is the sum of the two before it: 3 + 5 = 8, so 5 + 8 = 13.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 7s

## Q4 — Number Puzzle (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** If 4 + 5 = 20, 3 + 6 = 18, and 2 + 7 = 14, then 5 + 4 = ___?
- A) 9
- B) 18
- C) 20  ✅
- D) 25

**Answer:** C) 20
**Explanation:** The "+" secretly means multiply: 4×5=20, 3×6=18, 2×7=14, so 5×4 = 20.
**Tier:** Hard · **Countdown:** 8s

## Q5 — Figure Analogy (Nonverbal battery — render as shape icons)
**Prompt:** An EMPTY circle is to a FILLED circle as an EMPTY square is to ___?
- A) Filled square  ✅
- B) Empty square
- C) Filled circle
- D) Filled triangle

**Answer:** A) Filled square
**Explanation:** The relation is "get filled in" while the shape stays the same, so the empty square becomes a filled square.
**Tier:** Easy · **Countdown:** 5s
