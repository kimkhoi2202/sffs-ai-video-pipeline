# Rebus puzzle preview — mark each one

**How to review:** each puzzle has a `verdict:` line. Change the word to one of:

- `KEEP` — good, use it
- `CUT` — no
- `MAYBE` — on the fence / needs a tweak (add a note after it)

You can also just delete the whole block of any you hate. When you're done, save the file and
tell me — I'll pull all the KEEPs, balance the mechanics, and build carousels from those only.

**Tags:**

- `[text]` = renders in the current component as-is (letters/words only).
- `[needs art]` = wants a small graphic or a new component feature — more work, flag if you want these.

**How a rebus works (the short version):** the phrase is hidden in *how* the words/letters are
drawn — where they sit, how big, what's missing, what color, how they sound read aloud — not in
what they literally say. The trick line explains which of those it is.
