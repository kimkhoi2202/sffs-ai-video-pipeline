## Q1 — Verbal Classification (Verbal battery)
**Prompt:** Which one does NOT belong? Robin · Sparrow · Salmon · Eagle
- A) Robin
- B) Sparrow
- C) Salmon  ✅
- D) Eagle

**Answer:** C) Salmon
**Explanation:** Robin, sparrow, and eagle are birds; a salmon is a fish.
**Tier:** Easy · **Countdown:** time per `video/content/cogat-timing-difficulty.md`

## Q2 — Number Series (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** What comes next? 3, 6, 12, 24, ___
- A) 36
- B) 48  ✅
- C) 30
- D) 42

**Answer:** B) 48
**Explanation:** Each number doubles (×2): 3→6→12→24, so the next is 24 × 2 = 48.
**Tier:** Easy · **Countdown:** time per `video/content/cogat-timing-difficulty.md`

## Q14 — Number Analogy (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** 2 → 8, 3 → 27, 4 → 64, so 5 → ___?
- A) 100
- B) 125  ✅
- C) 81
- D) 216

**Answer:** B) 125
**Explanation:** Each number is cubed (n × n × n): 2³=8, 3³=27, 4³=64, so 5³ = 125.
**Tier:** Hard · **Countdown:** time per `video/content/cogat-timing-difficulty.md`

## Q15 — Number Puzzle (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** If 4 + 5 = 20, 3 + 6 = 18, and 2 + 7 = 14, then 5 + 4 = ___?
- A) 9
- B) 18
- C) 20  ✅
- D) 25

**Answer:** C) 20
**Explanation:** The "+" secretly means multiply: 4×5=20, 3×6=18, 2×7=14, so 5×4 = 20.
**Tier:** Hard · **Countdown:** time per `video/content/cogat-timing-difficulty.md`
