## Q1 — Verbal Analogy (Verbal battery)
**Prompt:** GIANT is to TINY as WIDE is to ___?
- A) Narrow  ✅
- B) Tall
- C) Long
- D) Big

**Answer:** A) Narrow
**Explanation:** Giant and tiny are opposites; the opposite of wide is narrow.
**Tier:** Easy · **Countdown:** 5s

## Q2 — Verbal Classification (Verbal battery)
**Prompt:** Which one does NOT belong? Add · Subtract · Multiply · Number
- A) Add
- B) Subtract
- C) Multiply
- D) Number  ✅

**Answer:** D) Number
**Explanation:** Add, subtract, and multiply are things you *do* (operations); a number is the thing you do them to.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 6s

## Q3 — Number Series (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** What comes next? 100, 92, 84, 76, ___
- A) 70
- B) 68  ✅
- C) 66
- D) 72

**Answer:** B) 68
**Explanation:** Each number goes down by 8: 100→92→84→76, so 76 − 8 = 68.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 6s

## Q4 — Number Analogy (Quantitative battery)
**Prompt:** 2 → 8, 3 → 27, 4 → 64, so 5 → ___?
- A) 100
- B) 125  ✅
- C) 81
- D) 216

**Answer:** B) 125
**Explanation:** Each number is cubed (n × n × n): 2³=8, 3³=27, 4³=64, so 5³ = 125.
**Tier:** Hard · **Countdown:** 8s

## Q5 — Figure Series / Position (Nonverbal battery — render as icons)
**Prompt:** A dot moves clockwise around the corners of a square: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, ___. Where is it next?
- A) Top-left
- B) Bottom-left  ✅
- C) Top-right
- D) Center

**Answer:** B) Bottom-left
**Explanation:** The dot steps clockwise corner to corner; after bottom-right the next clockwise corner is bottom-left.
**Tier:** Medium · **Countdown:** 7s
