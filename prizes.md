# Prize Mechanic — $500 Input Tier + $2,000 Spotlight

The two-tier prize that rewards participation, drives the parent-email capture, and — by **publicly
documenting winners** — generates social proof that feeds back into the Kid Loop.

> **Read with:** [`compliance.md`](compliance.md) §5 (prize legal, COPPA/CARU) ·
> [`riddle-video-style-spec.md`](riddle-video-style-spec.md) §0 & §11.10 (on-screen prize framing) ·
> [`repurposing.md`](repurposing.md) (winner content across surfaces).
>
> **Not legal advice.** Sweepstakes/contest law is state-specific. Have counsel draft the **Official
> Rules** and confirm registration/bonding where required **before** promoting any prize.

---

## 0. The two tiers at a glance

| Tier | Amount | Purpose | Selection (default — set in Official Rules) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Input / participation** | **$500** | Reward entering the funnel; high-frequency wins keep the loop warm | **Random draw** among all valid parent entries in the period |
| **Spotlight** | **$2,000** | Flagship reward; the "hero win" that anchors social proof | Larger random draw **or** judged/editorial pick — **must be disclosed** |

Both are **publicly documented** (rules posted, winners announced) per the PRD — the documentation is
itself a growth asset (§4).

---

## 1. How entry works (parent-email = the entry)

Entry is the **same action as the funnel conversion**: a **parent** enters **their** email on the
gate. No separate purchase, no child data.

```
Kid watches video ─► "Ask a grown-up to enter their email to unlock your results"
                  ─► Parent enters THEIR email on the gate (consent + rules linked)
                  ─► That email = 1 valid entry for the current prize period
                  ─► Results/score unlocked; parent notified if selected
```

- **No purchase necessary.** Ever. (Required for a lawful sweepstakes.)
- **Parent is the entrant and the recipient/custodian** — 18+ / age of majority; enters on behalf of
  the child. The **child is never the contracting party** and never needs to give PII to win.
- **One entry per household per period** (recommended, to keep it fair and simplify §5 tax tracking).
  Define "household" in the rules.
- **Eligibility** (set in rules): geography (e.g. US only), parent age, exclusions. **Void where prohibited.**

---

## 2. Cadence (default — confirm in Official Rules)

| Tier | Suggested cadence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **$500 input** | **Monthly** draw | Frequent wins = steady social-proof cadence + reason to re-enter |
| **$2,000 spotlight** | **Quarterly** draw | Bigger moment; anchor a flagship video + press-style announcement |

Publish the **entry window, draw date, and notification window** for each period in the rules and on
the gate. Keep odds statements honest ("odds depend on number of entries").

---

## 3. Selection → notification → payout

1. **Close the entry window;** compile valid entries (parent emails, de-duped per household).
2. **Select** per the disclosed method (random draw = auditable RNG; spotlight judged = disclosed
   criteria + panel). Record the process.
3. **Notify the parent** by email; require a **claim reply** within a stated window (e.g. 7 days) or
   an alternate is drawn.
4. **Verify eligibility** (parent 18+, geography, one-per-household).
5. **Tax paperwork before payout for ≥ $600** (see §5): collect **W-9** from the parent.
6. **Pay the parent** (the named recipient/custodian) via the stated method.
7. **Get publicity consent** (§4) — a separate, explicit opt-in — before featuring the winner.
8. **Document the winner publicly** (§4) and log everything.

## 4. Announce + publicly document winners (the social-proof loop)

Public winner documentation satisfies the PRD's "publicly documented" requirement **and** doubles as
social proof — but it must stay **PII-safe and CARU-safe**.

### What you may show (with explicit, separate parent consent)

- **Preferred (safest):** the **prize + a parent-facing quote/testimonial** and a **city/state or
  region** — no child identifiers. ("A parent from Ohio unlocked the $2,000 Spotlight!")
- **Parent's first name / initial**, with written consent.
- **Child's first name only**, or an anonymized handle — **only** with explicit written parental
  consent, and **never** age/school/location/contact. Prefer to keep the child fully anonymous.
- **No child photo/voice/full name** unless separately, explicitly consented in writing — default is
  **don't**. When in doubt, feature the **parent** or keep it anonymous.

### Where winners are documented

| Surface | Format (brand) |
|---|---|
| **Winners page** (linked from gate + rules) | Bordered, hard-shadow cards: period, tier, region, parent quote. Ongoing public record. |
| **Video shout-out** | End-card/segment: Anton "LAST MONTH'S WINNER" on an accent block, parent-facing, links to public rules. |
| **Social** (Shorts/carousel/blog) | Reuse the [`repurposing.md`](repurposing.md) formats: a "we paid out $X this month" brand card + parent quote. |
| **Newsletter to parents** | Parent list only: winner recap + next draw date. |

**Framing rules:** parent-facing, truthful, no "you'll definitely win," no pressure/urgency, no
"nag/tag friends," non-shaming. Show **real, honest amounts** ($500 / $2,000) and link the rules.

## 5. Prize legal notes

- **Parent = named recipient/custodian.** Prize awarded to the parent/guardian who entered, on behalf
  of the child. Child gives **no PII** to claim.
- **W-9 + 1099 for prizes ≥ $600.** For any prize valued **≥ $600 to one household in a calendar
  year**, collect **Form W-9** (TIN) from the **parent** before payout and issue **Form 1099-MISC**.
  - **$2,000 Spotlight → always** triggers W-9 + 1099.
  - **$500 inputs → track per household across the year**; **two** $500 wins to one household hits
    $1,000 and triggers the 1099 (one-per-household-per-period plus annual tracking keeps this clean).
  - Winner is responsible for taxes; state this in the rules.
- **Official Rules (public, linked everywhere prizes appear):** sponsor identity (**neutral Closer
  entity — NOT Alpha School / Alpha AI**), eligibility, **no purchase necessary**, **void where
  prohibited**, entry method (parent email) + limit, selection method + **odds**, prize description +
  **ARV**, key dates, notification/claim process, publicity/consent terms, privacy reference, tax
  responsibility, and any state registration/bonding disclosures.
- **CARU/COPPA alignment:** entry is a **parent** action; no child data collected to enter or win;
  prize copy is parent-facing and non-manipulative (see [`compliance.md`](compliance.md)).
- **No Alpha branding** on any prize surface (rules, winners page, announcements, checks/payouts).

---

## 6. On-screen + gate presentation

- **In-video (parent-facing):** a bordered pill / lower-third or end card — "Grown-ups: enter your
  email to unlock the score **+ enter this month's $500 & $2,000 prizes**. Rules: [URL]." Anton on an
  accent/`ink` block, hard shadow, links to **public rules**. (Spec §11.10.)
- **On the gate:** brief parent-facing prize line + **link to Official Rules** next to the consent
  line. No child fields. (See [`compliance.md`](compliance.md) §2.)
- **Never** imply the child must do anything to win, or that winning is guaranteed/easy.

---

## 7. Prize checklist (per period)

- [ ] **Official Rules** finalized by counsel, **posted publicly**, and linked from gate + every prize mention.
- [ ] Rules state: **no purchase necessary**, **void where prohibited**, eligibility, entry limit,
      selection method + **odds**, ARV, dates, **parent as recipient/custodian**, tax responsibility.
- [ ] **No Alpha** branding on any prize surface.
- [ ] Entry = **parent email only**; **zero child PII** collected to enter or claim.
- [ ] Selection method executed + **recorded/auditable**; alternate-winner process ready.
- [ ] **W-9 collected** from the parent for any payout **≥ $600** (spotlight always; track stacked $500s).
- [ ] **1099-MISC** issued for qualifying payouts; per-household annual tracking maintained.
- [ ] **Separate publicity consent** obtained before featuring a winner; child kept anonymous by default.
- [ ] Winner documented on the **winners page** + announced **CARU-safely** (honest, parent-facing, non-scary).
- [ ] Payout completed to the **parent**; full record logged.
