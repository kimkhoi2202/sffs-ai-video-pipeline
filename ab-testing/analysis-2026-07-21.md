# First-look analysis — 2026-07-21

**Scope:** 14 published posts (2026-07-19 → 2026-07-21), 10 with metrics from the Publer
analytics screenshot (page 1 of 2), 4 `pending`. Source of truth: `ab-database.json`.

## ⚠️ Read this before trusting anything below

This is **not statistically meaningful.** Treat every statement here as a hypothesis to test
at scale, not a result.

- **Tiny sample:** 1–2 posts per variant; several families (`dont-narrate`, `speed`,
  `one-question`, `mascot`) have **zero** posts. `no-answer` has exactly one, and its label is
  even uncertain.
- **Reach varies ~250×** (from **4** to **991**). With that spread, a single lucky or throttled
  post swamps any variant effect.
- **Brand-new accounts / cold start:** the algorithm is still calibrating; near-zero follower
  base makes TikTok's `reach_rate` values garbage (e.g. `"3.1K%"`, `"3.5K%"`).
- **Confounds everywhere:** platform, posting time-of-day, hashtag set (varies per post), and
  order of posting are all mixed in with the variant.
- **4 posts have no metrics yet**, including the newest cliffhanger and both brand promos.

**Conclusion up front:** we cannot name a winning variant. We can only name the biggest
_confound_ to control for (platform) and a couple of things worth deliberately testing once the
automated loop is posting many videos per family.

## Cautious hypotheses (to test at scale, not to act on yet)

1. **Platform is the dominant lever right now — and the biggest confound.**
   For identical videos, TikTok pulled multiples of IG's reach (up to ~9×) and ~3–6% eng vs
   IG's ~0–0.9%. Any variant comparison that mixes platforms is currently measuring platform,
   not the variant. **Action:** the loop must compare variants **within a single platform**,
   and probably weight TikTok for reach experiments while it's this lopsided.

2. **Leading with "odd one out" (package 01) is the current high-water mark — but n=1.**
   The single best post (991 reach, 5.55% eng) opened on an easy, everyone-can-try verbal
   "odd one out" question. Weak, testable idea: an instantly-attemptable first question may hook
   more viewers than opening on "word analogy." Its IG twin only did 113/0.88%, so most of the
   999-reach story is still platform + algorithm variance. **Action:** A/B first-question type
   (odd-one-out vs word-analogy) within TikTok, many reps.

3. **No evidence yet that "intro" or the "comment-for-answer" mechanic helps.**
   - *Intro:* the only WITHINTRO posts are package 03; its TikTok post was undelivered
     (reach 4) and its IG post was middle-of-the-pack (128 / 0.78%). Inconclusive — and a ~6s
     brand lead-in is exactly the kind of slow open a retention-hungry algorithm can punish, so
     it's worth an explicit intro-vs-cold-open test.
   - *Cliffhanger / no-answer:* these withhold the answer and ask viewers to "comment your
     answer," yet produced **0 comments** (the only comment in the whole dataset was on a
     _standard_ IG post). At this reach, comments are just rare — but there's no early signal the
     mechanic drives them. **Action:** test withheld-answer vs full-reveal within TikTok and
     watch comments + saves specifically.

## What would make this trustworthy

Run the automated loop until each variant family has **many tens of posts per platform**,
ideally randomizing posting time and holding hashtags/thumbnail constant, so platform,
time-of-day, and cold-start noise average out. Then revisit these three hypotheses with
per-platform medians (not means — the reach=4 and reach=991 posts show why the mean lies).
